# 6-Part Landing Page Structure (order is important):

## Part 1 — Headline + Sub-headline (Answer: Dream Outcome)

- Headline
- Sub-headline

This answers question #1: "What do I get?"

## Part 2 — The "Before vs. After" (Answer: Dream Outcome + Time Delay)

- Show two columns: BEFORE (chaotic, 30 minutes searching for documents, back and forth emails) vs. AFTER (everything indexed, search takes 2 seconds, external users can access it themselves).

This answers: "How long does it take?" — You show the difference visually.

## Part 3 — The "How It Works" (Answer: Effort & Sacrifice)

- 3 simple steps: (1) Run 1 command in Terminal (include a screenshot), (2) Open a browser, (3) All documents organized.
- This is critical: Don't hide the Terminal. Instead, show that it's only 1 line and the rest is done through the browser.
- Add a sentence like: "Need help? We can do a free remote installation." — this lowers effort.

## Part 4 — Social Proof (Answer: Perceived Likelihood)

- This is the part where you overcome your biggest objections.
- Testimonials from non-IT people — for example: "I'm not a technical person. But I just copied and pasted 1 command and everything worked. Now my team never asks 'where's the file?' anymore."
- If there aren't any testimonials, replace them with a 60-second demo video showing the process from start to finish.
- Guarantee: "30-day money-back guarantee — if the installation fails or doesn't work, we'll refund you."

## Part 5 — The Offer + Pricing (Answer: Price)

- Display a price of $49–$97.
- Add a bonus to boost perceived value: for example, "Free 1-on-1 setup call" or "Document structure template for 5 divisions."
- This is the Stacking.

## Part 6 — Call to Action (CTA)

- Button: "Get Started Now — 30-Day Guarantee"
- Below the button: "5-minute installation. If you get stuck, we'll help."
