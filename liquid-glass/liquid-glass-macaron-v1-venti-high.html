01 / Refraction

Light bends here

Backdrop pixels are blurred, saturated, and lifted — never flattened. What sits behind the pane stays legible as light, the way a macaron shell reveals the filling beneath.

02 / Specularity

Edges catch the sun

A gradient-masked rim brightens the upper boundary the way a real lens flares along its bevel. The border is light, not a line.

03 / Responsivity

The pane remembers

A warm radial glow tracks your cursor through each surface, and a caustic sheen sweeps across on approach — glass behaving like glass.

04 / The recipe

Anatomy of a sweet pane

backdrop-filter: blur(20px) saturate(1.45) brightness(1.02) is only the beginning. Inner shadows carve a concave bowl; a masked gradient paints the specular rim; a ::after layer renders cursor light via custom properties; and a diagonal sheen — the caustic — passes over on hover. Four stacked illusions, zero images.

The macaron floating beside the hero is the same primitive — two domed lenses around a cream filling, refracting the pastels behind them.
