04 / The recipe

Anatomy of a liquid pane

It starts with feTurbulence feeding a feDisplacementMap that nudges the backdrop by a few dozen pixels. Over that: backdrop-filter: blur(14px) saturate(1.7) for body, an inner shadow bowl for depth, a mask-composited gradient for the specular rim, a cursor-driven radial glow, and a diagonal caustic that passes on hover.

Six stacked illusions, zero raster images. Depth from restraint, not from piling blur on blur.
