fig. 03 — the recipe

Five layers, one pane.

Every surface on this bench shares a single primitive. Depth comes from restraint, not from stacking blur on blur.

01 / The bath

Everything behind is washed in light

Backdrop pixels are blurred, saturated, and lifted — never flattened. The scene stays legible as color and glow, never as noise, so the pane reads as a material sitting in front of the world.

backdrop-filter: blur(18px) saturate(1.7) brightness(1.04);
02 / The bowl

A carved meniscus

Three inset shadows hollow a shallow concave bowl — thicker at the edges, thinner at the center, like a lens blank fresh from the grinder.

03 / The rim

The edge is lit, not drawn

A gradient-masked border paints specular light along the upper bevel, the way real glass flares where it meets the sun.

04 / The memory

The pane notices you

Each surface tracks your pointer through --mx --my custom properties and answers with a soft radial glow; approach it and a caustic sheen sweeps across like light over moving water.

05 / poured,
not placed.

Panels rise on long expo curves, staggered like cooling glass. The print scene behind never stops drifting, the lens bobs on its mount, and the grain flickers a single frame at a time — liquid is a state, not a style.
